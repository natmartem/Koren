Thank you for helping!

  1. Tap the red button, say the word once, tap again to stop. It plays back so you can check it.
  2. Read exactly what is written, at a normal everyday pace. The accent mark shows the stressed syllable; say the word naturally.
  3. Sentences: say them as you would to a friend, not slowly and not like a dictation.
  4. Not happy? Just record again. Everything is saved on this phone as you go, so you can stop and come back later.
  5. When you're done (or done for today), tap Send recordings at the bottom and send the file to the person who gave you this link.

Your recordings stay yours. They are used only inside this learning app, are not covered by the app's open licenses, and won't be reused elsewhere without asking you.

Moving to another phone?

Load a recordings file you sent earlier to continue where you left off.
